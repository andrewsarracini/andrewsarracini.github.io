About

I'm a data scientist who builds. As Head of Technology at Keyspire, I'm the sole technical lead: I take problems straight from coaches, operations, and leadership, decide what to build, and own it in production, from LLM agents to the data pipelines the business runs on. I measure model behavior instead of guessing at it.
